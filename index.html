Four disciplines, one shipment.

Procurement

Workstations, tablets, printers, cash drawers, and every cable in between — sourced against exact part numbers and specified to the project, not substituted on the fly.

Movement

03 — How it moves

From purchase order to handover protocol.

  1. 01

    Purchase order confirmed

    Scope, part numbers, and quantities are locked against the commercial proposal before anything is ordered.

  2. 02

    Cleared through customs

    Import VAT is settled, SAL and handling agents coordinated, and the shipment released to the delivery address.

  3. 03

    Installed and handed over

    Hardware is installed on site and signed off against the handover protocol — the system is live, not just delivered.

  4. 04

    Reconciled and settled

    Costs are itemised and reconciled — quarterly where VAT recovery applies — and a clean settlement is issued to every party.
